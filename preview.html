⚡ Gmail Quick Start
  1. Click the golden “Copy for Gmail Compose” button at top.
  2. Open Gmail and click Compose.
  3. Click inside the email body and press Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on Mac).
  4. The header logo, brand name, and centered layout will appear immediately!
  5. Click into the middle text in Gmail to edit your message.
  6. Optional: Click ⋮ → Templates → Save draft as template to save in Gmail permanently!
