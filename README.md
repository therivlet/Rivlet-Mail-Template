## 🚀 How to Use in Gmail (Fastest Method)

1. Open **[`preview.html`](file:///c:/Users/thari/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/Rivlet-Mail-Template/preview.html)** in your web browser.
2. Select your desired occasion (**General**, **VIP Launch**, **Founder’s Letter**, **B2B**, **Order Concierge**, **Run Club**).
3. Click the golden **“📋 Copy for Gmail Compose”** button at the top right.
4. Open [Gmail](https://mail.google.com) and click **Compose**.
5. Click inside the email body and press **Ctrl + V** (`Cmd + V` on Mac).
6. Edit your greeting, message, or specifics directly in Gmail!

---

## 💾 Saving as Permanent Gmail Templates

1. In the Gmail compose window, click the **⋮ (More options)** icon in the bottom-right toolbar.
2. Hover over **Templates** → **Save draft as template** → **Save as new template**.
3. Give it a name (e.g., `Rivlet — VIP Drop` or `Rivlet — General`).
4. To use in the future, just select it from **Templates** in Gmail!
